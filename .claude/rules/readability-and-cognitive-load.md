# Readability and Cognitive Load

- Keep functions small and intention-revealing.
- Prefer early returns to deep nesting.
- Name intermediate booleans by business meaning.
- Extract cohesive helpers instead of long mixed-purpose blocks.
- Keep related logic local and avoid hidden control flow.
